About

This portfolio reflects two passions — piano and astronomy. Music provides rhythm and structure; astronomy fuels curiosity and perspective. Together, they remind me that great code, like art, is a harmony of logic and creativity.

  1. 01 · 2020–2023

    Foundation

    Built a foundation in Computer Science at Anglia Ruskin University, exploring algorithms, data structures, and full-stack development. Discovered that clean logic can be as elegant as music.

  2. 02 · Along the way

    Exploration

    Through collaborative projects, I learned to merge creativity with engineering, designing efficient, expressive systems. Every challenge felt like composing a new melody in code.

  3. 03 · 2024–2025
